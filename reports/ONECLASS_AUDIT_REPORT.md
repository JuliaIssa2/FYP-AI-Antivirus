## 1. Mathematical verification

### Spectral features

For thin QR factorizations, write $B=Q_B R_B$ and $A^T=Q_A R_A$. Then $BA=Q_B(R_B R_A^T)Q_A^T$. The outer factors have orthonormal columns, so the rank-sized core has the same singular values as the dense update after omitting the extra structural zeros. The core can itself have zero singular values when effective rank drops. At the supported rank 16 and hidden size 4096, this is a 16 × 16 SVD. The code uses float64 here after the shared loader casts to float32. [features.py, lines 32–43](https://github.com/JuliaIssa2/FYP-AI-Antivirus/blob/c369f4b1017214f777157e62638b7a9dd6c4b739/shared_protocol/detectors/oneclass/features.py#L32-L43)

| Per-layer statistic | Definition and audit verdict |
|---|---|
| Largest singular value | First singular value of the core SVD. Correct for the specified orientation. |
| Frobenius norm | Square root of summed squared singular values, with an added $10^{-30}$ energy floor. Correct as implemented and documented, but the exact mathematical norm of zero is zero. |
| Energy concentration | Largest squared singular value divided by total energy plus the floor. Correct for the stated regularized definition. |
| Spectral entropy | Energy fractions times their negative log, divided by log of factor rank, with regularization. Correct as a rank-normalized, epsilon-adjusted convention. Rank-deficient spectra retain zeros and normalize by configured factor rank, not effective rank. |
| Excess kurtosis | Fourth centered moment divided by population variance squared, minus three. Correct for nonconstant spectra in ordinary numerical ranges. An exactly constant spectrum is assigned zero by convention. |

For **each** of these five features, Q and V are summarized separately over 32 layers using arithmetic mean, population standard deviation and maximum. Ordering is projection, statistic, aggregation, giving **2 × 5 × 3 = 30**. This is a summary of the unscaled update; multiplying by alpha/r would change magnitude features. Omitting that explicit multiplier does not remove training-recipe information from the learned factors. [features.py, lines 1–14 and 21–57](https://github.com/JuliaIssa2/FYP-AI-Antivirus/blob/c369f4b1017214f777157e62638b7a9dd6c4b739/shared_protocol/detectors/oneclass/features.py#L21-L57)

### Benign reference and scoring

`LedoitWolf().fit(x)` estimates the benign mean and mixes empirical covariance $S$ with $\operatorname{tr}(S)I/30$. Its estimated coefficient controls how much to shrink toward that target. The saved precision $P$ yields $(x-\mu)^T P(x-\mu)$. This is **squared** Mahalanobis distance and higher means farther from the benign reference. The code checks the saved mean and precision dimensions and finiteness, and checks precision symmetry and positive definiteness. Validation AUROC is computed after fitting and does not update those parameters. The squared score does not require a Gaussian probability interpretation. [Ledoit–Wolf reference](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.covariance.LedoitWolf.html) · [train.py, lines 87–112](https://github.com/JuliaIssa2/FYP-AI-Antivirus/blob/c369f4b1017214f777157e62638b7a9dd6c4b739/shared_protocol/detectors/oneclass/train.py#L87-L112) · [model.py, lines 22–45](https://github.com/JuliaIssa2/FYP-AI-Antivirus/blob/c369f4b1017214f777157e62638b7a9dd6c4b739/shared_protocol/detectors/oneclass/model.py#L22-L45)

## 3. Shared protocol and scientific validity

| Stage | Verified from source | Evidence required before formal use |
|---|---|---|
| Fit and validation | Verified partition records are checked against binding commitments before the benign filter, then only selected fit files are resolved and hashed. Validation is an optional post-fit AUROC diagnostic that may include both labels. Saved fit IDs must exactly match benign train records and tuning IDs must be empty. | Validate labels and decoys, prove cache provenance, confirm source-row and lineage evidence and keep diagnostic results from informing post-freeze choices. Compare repeated fits with and without validation and assert identical learned parameters, allowing diagnostic metadata to differ. [OC train.py:73–112; PG train.py:95–120; SP scoring.py:127–136] |
| Split and calibration | Split construction keeps declared leakage groups intact. Calibration selects one lexicographic representative per group and requires benign records. The order statistic is `ceil(0.95*(n+1))`, uses strict `>`, and returns infinity below 19 representatives. It binds scores to the protocol/spec. | Verify group independence and representative policy before calibration. Confirm the threshold and model were frozen before evaluation. Report achieved FPR under shift instead of interpreting 5% as a guarantee. Synthetic tests should cover 18/19, ties, wrong membership and evaluation-label isolation. [SP splits.py:59–141; SP scoring.py:99–108,258–296; SP calibration.py:14–22,47–79] |
| Evaluation and final holdout | Regular evaluation checks the frozen detector spec and evaluation partition. It reports reserved task/family/payload slices and reservation companions separately. The final API requires a distinct release record. | Use released views and independent artifact custody. Keep final data sealed during all corrections and exploratory analysis. Quantify group support and attach uncertainty suited to the estimand. [SP evaluation.py:17–89; SP final_evaluation.py:41–86] |

**Real-data questions, separate from defects.** The detector models a single benign center and covariance. Several benign tasks may form separate clusters, and layer mean/std/max can hide a localized signal. Rank, recipe, training seed, alpha effects on learned factors, and shared source examples may drive distance. Covariance shrinkage stabilizes estimation but cannot remove such confounding. The scientific test is whether the frozen method flags malicious adapters at useful recall while controlling observed false alarms on new benign tasks. Compare against the shared-split supervised baseline with the same partitions and calibration policy. To isolate the effect of benign-only training, a matched-feature comparator would be needed. Otherwise differences reflect feature representation and detector as well as supervision. MNTD's result concerns its own query-based baseline. Report a scoped LoRA finding rather than a conclusion about all one-class methods. [MNTD, §IV-B and Tables III and VII](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.03137v4)

5. **P2 · Preregister real-data validation.** After correction acceptance and a verified manifest/split, define useful recall and FPR, independent groups and acceptable interval support. Run matched and shifted comparisons with frozen thresholds, using authorized evaluation only. Interpret group-level differences before requesting a separate final assessment release.
